# Heart Quest

A browser dating simulator. 12 characters. 10 locations. 80+ actions. One goal.

## [>>> PLAY NOW <<<](https://hulkiokantabak.github.io/heart-quest/)

No download needed — runs in your browser on desktop and mobile.

## How to Play

- Create your character — pick your name, gender, traits, and interests (or hit ⚡ Quick Start)
- Choose a location and approach one of 12 romanceable characters
- Take actions, write custom dialogue, or pick from suggestions
- Build the relationship from Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Love Interest → Partner
- Reach 100 relationship points to complete the quest — but a single encounter can only move things so far, so it takes a few good moments to get there

## Features

- 12 characters with distinct personalities, preferences, and memory
- 10 locations, each with its own actions; time of day affects who you'll click with
- Custom action input — write anything, the game analyses your tone (and knows "not boring" is a compliment)
- Character memory system — they remember what you said and did, and warm to you faster as trust builds
- Variety matters — leaning on the same move twice lands softer
- Achievement system with unlock criteria
- 8 turns per game session
- Keyboard-playable and screen-reader friendly
- Progress saved automatically
